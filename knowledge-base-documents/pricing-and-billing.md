# Harbor: Pricing and Billing

> Fictional reference material for the hiring assignment. Harbor is not a real product. All prices and policies in this document are invented for this exercise.

## Plans

Prices are in USD, exclude applicable taxes, and apply per workspace. There are no per-user charges within the included member limit. Owners, editors, and viewers all count toward that limit; pending invitations do not count until accepted.

| Plan     | Monthly billing | Annual billing | Members | Stored feedback records | Slack integration |
| -------- | --------------: | -------------: | ------: | ----------------------: | ----------------- |
| Starter  |              $0 |             $0 |       3 |                     200 | No                |
| Team     |   $24 per month |  $240 per year |      15 |                   5,000 | Yes               |
| Business |   $79 per month |  $790 per year |      50 |                  25,000 | Yes               |

All plans include collections and a private roadmap. Public roadmap sharing is available on Team and Business only.

The feedback limit applies to records currently stored in the workspace, not to records created during a month. Deleting a record frees capacity. There are no automatic overage charges: when a limit is reached, new members or feedback records cannot be added until capacity is freed or the plan is upgraded.

## Annual billing

Annual subscriptions are charged upfront for 12 months. Team costs $288 for 12 monthly payments versus $240 annually, a saving of $48. Business costs $948 for 12 monthly payments versus $790 annually, a saving of $158.

Annual pricing is not a monthly installment plan.

## Trial

New workspaces can activate a 14-day Team trial without a payment card. The trial includes Team features and limits.

At the end of the trial, the workspace returns to Starter unless an owner purchases a paid subscription. No automatic charge occurs without a purchase.

If the workspace exceeds Starter limits, existing content remains readable, but adding feedback or members is blocked until the workspace is within the limits or upgrades. Public roadmap sharing and the Slack integration are disabled.

## Plan changes

Upgrades take effect immediately. The unused portion of the current paid subscription is credited against the prorated cost of the new plan for the remainder of the billing period.

Downgrades take effect at the next renewal. If the workspace exceeds the new plan's limits, existing content remains readable, but adding feedback or members is blocked until it is within the limits. Existing records are not automatically deleted. Features unavailable on the new plan are disabled when the downgrade takes effect.

There is no custom plan or self-service purchase of additional member capacity in this exercise. A workspace needing more than 50 members is not supported by the listed plans.

## Cancellation and refunds

Only a workspace owner can change or cancel a subscription.

Cancellation stops the next renewal. Paid features remain available until the end of the current billing period, after which the workspace moves to Starter under the same limit rules as a downgrade.

Canceling a subscription does not delete the workspace or its data.

A refund may be requested within seven calendar days of the first paid purchase. Renewals and partially used billing periods are not refundable under this policy. Refund requests must be sent to the support email listed in the support document.

## Payments and invoices

Harbor accepts credit and debit cards. Bank transfers, purchase orders, and cryptocurrency are not supported.

Owners can download invoices from workspace billing settings. Editors and viewers cannot access invoices or change payment details.

This document does not specify country-specific tax rates, currency conversion fees, or negotiated discounts. Do not infer those details from the listed prices.
